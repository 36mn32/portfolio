# Mechanical Engineering Technology Portfolio

A responsive, static portfolio website with pages for Home, About Me, Projects, Skills, and Contact.

## View locally

Open `index.html` in a web browser. The site uses plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and does not require a build step or dependencies.

## Personalize

- Replace the About Me placeholder with your introduction.
- Update the three project cards and replace their illustrations in `images/` with project photos or renders.
- Adjust the skills and proficiency labels to match your experience. Each skill expands when clicked or activated with the keyboard; replace its placeholder paragraph in `skills.html` with your description.
- Add your preferred contact information on the Contact page. The site has no backend, so it does not include a message-sending form.
## Current content

The portfolio includes Max's introduction, NJIT contact details, customized skill descriptions, and Onshape and SolidWorks project images in the project gallery. Some sections still contain placeholders for future updates.

The home page links directly to the About Me and Projects pages. About Me describes involvement in NJIT FormulaSAE and ASME, and the Skills page includes descriptions of materials, metrology, and engineering mechanics. The Contact page includes a phone-number placeholder.

Every page includes a footer with a **Contact me** link to the Contact page.

The **Dark mode** button in every page header switches between light and dark colors, including text and cards. Your choice is saved in browser storage and restored across pages and visits when storage is available. Without JavaScript, the site keeps its light appearance.

The project gallery includes an angled CAD plate and a stop-base assembly image (cadproj2.png) in place of the engineering-analysis illustration.

On screens up to 760px wide, project cards stack the full image above the description. Images keep their original proportions, and long titles wrap within the card. Larger screens show uncropped images inside consistent image frames.
